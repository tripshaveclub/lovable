# TRIP SHAVE CLUB — Bootstrap Business Plan
## Starting Capital: $50,000 | Market: USA | Model: B2C First → B2B
### Smooth. Confident. You.

---

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Trip Shave Club is a personal care brand built around the world's first
gel-integrated razor — a 5-blade razor with a built-in 20ml Aloe Vera
gel reservoir. No shaving cream needed. One product. Complete shave.

- **Starting Capital:** $50,000 (self-funded)
- **Manufacturing:** Turkey — 11 million units annual capacity
- **Year 1 Strategy:** Amazon FBA + TikTok/Instagram
- **Year 1 Revenue Target:** $280,000
- **Break-even:** Month 8
- **Year 3 Revenue Target:** $2.8M
- **Path to Growth:** Bootstrap → Profitable → Raise at better valuation

---

## WHY $50K IS ENOUGH TO START

Most consumer brands fail because they raise too much and spend wrong.
With $50K and a manufacturer already in place, Trip Shave Club has the
single biggest advantage: **zero product development cost.**

The factory exists. The product is tested. The capacity is there.
We just need to sell.

## PRODUCT & PRICING STRATEGY

### SKUs to Launch First (Keep it Simple)

| SKU | Product | Retail Price | Amazon Price | COGS |
|-----|---------|-------------|--------------|------|
| TSC-W1 | Women's Gel Razor (Pink) | $12.99 | $10.99 | $1.20 |
| TSC-M1 | Men's Gel Razor (Black) | $12.99 | $10.99 | $1.20 |
| TSC-3PK | 3-Pack (Women's) | $28.99 | $25.99 | $3.50 |

**Rule: Start with 1 SKU (Women's). Add Men's at Month 3.**

Women's razor is:
- Higher price tolerance
- More likely to share on social media
- Less competitive on Amazon vs men's

---

## AMAZON STRATEGY — STEP BY STEP

1. **Month 1:** List product, get 10–15 reviews from friends/family
2. **Month 2:** Turn on PPC — auto campaigns first, then manual
3. **Month 3:** Optimize listing with A+ Content (brand story, comparison chart)
4. **Month 4:** Launch 3-pack ASIN to increase average order value
5. **Month 5:** Apply for Amazon Launchpad or Amazon Climate Pledge Friendly
6. **Month 6:** Subscribe & Save program — builds recurring revenue

**Key Amazon KPIs to track:**
- Conversion Rate: target >15%
- ACOS: keep under 35%
- Review Rate: aim for 1 review per 50 orders
- BSR (Best Seller Rank): target top 200 in "Women's Razors"

## COMPETITIVE POSITIONING

With $50K you can NOT compete with Gillette on ads.
You compete on **story, authenticity, and innovation.**

**Your unfair advantages:**
- You ARE the manufacturer — no middleman, 70%+ margins
- The product genuinely solves a real problem (no cream needed)
- Turkey manufacturing = strong USD cost advantage
- Dermatologically tested = credibility
- The "built-in gel" concept is highly shareable/viral

## RISK MANAGEMENT

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|
| Slow Amazon sales | TikTok organic as backup channel |
| Inventory stuck in customs | Buffer 2-week extra lead time |
| Negative reviews | Include insert card asking for feedback first |
| Cash flow gap | Don't reorder until 30% of stock remains |
| Competitor copies product | File US trademark in Month 3 |
| TikTok ban | Build email list from Day 1 as owned channel |
