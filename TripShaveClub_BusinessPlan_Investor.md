- Shaving requires multiple products: razor + cream/gel/foam = extra cost, mess, and time
- Traditional razors cause skin irritation, dryness, and razor burn

- Travelers and on-the-go users struggle with carrying multiple products
- The $4.5B US shaving market is dominated by legacy brands with outdated formats

- **Built-in 20ml Aloe Vera Gel Reservoir** — no foam, no cream needed
- **5-Blade Precision System** — ultra-close, smooth shave
- **Press-to-Release Gel Technology** — controlled dispensing with every stroke
- **Dermatologically Tested** — safe for all skin types
- **Moisturizes, Soothes & Protects** skin during and after shaving
- Designed for both men and women
- Eco-friendly: fewer products = less packaging waste
